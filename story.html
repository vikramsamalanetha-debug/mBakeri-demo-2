The person behind the M

Travel became taste.

Chris grew up in Vero Beach, then moved away and immersed himself in other cultures. New York, travel, fashion, live entertainment, design and hospitality became part of the same education.

Instead of building a bakery around one inherited formula, he built it around instinct: notice what is beautiful, understand why it works, then make something personal from it.

That is why M Bakeri feels closer to a creative studio than a conventional bakery.

Food is fashion. It moves, changes, borrows, surprises — and gives people a reason to come back curious.
